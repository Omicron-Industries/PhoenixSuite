Minecraft Forge 1.20.1

A suite of mods, one shared foundation.

The PhoenixSuite is a collection of minecraft mods and modpacks focused on stability, ease of use, good documentation, and community focus. Most of the mods in the Suite are modpack utility and not content mods. This Suite is built and maintained by Phoenixvine.

What's in the suite

🧩

A shared foundation

PhoenixCore underpins most of the suite — a common set of systems most other Phoenix mods build on, instead of each reinventing the same plumbing.

📜

A real quest system

Phoenix Chronicles brings modern quest editing — live dependency lines, drag-to-reorder, composable filters — with direct FTB Quests import support.

🎒

A complete modpack

Phoenix Forge Technologies is the flagship pack built around the suite — the proving ground where everything gets used together.

📚

Actually documented

A shared wiki for the whole suite — player guides, modpack-building guides, and developer docs for anyone extending the mods themselves.
